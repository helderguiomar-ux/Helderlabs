# Landing Page Repositioning Review - HELDERLABS ERP

Detailed review of the Landing Page adjustments under RC2.1 branding directives.

---

## 📈 Executive Role Evaluations

### 1. CEO / COO Perspective
- **Impression**: Immediate business value focus. Clear messaging on integration, manual task reduction, and multi-company support. Low-level tech details (Event Bus, headers) are hidden below the fold under technical infrastructure, ensuring a business-value-first read.

### 2. CFO Perspective
- **Impression**: Immediate cost-saving value visible. The benefits section clearly outlines consolidation of multiple SaaS subscriptions and reduced operational costs.

### 3. CIO / IT Director Perspective
- **Impression**: Security and scalability sections outline enterprise readiness, encrypted databases, RBAC controls, and dynamic performance scalability.

---

## 🔍 SEO & Microcopy Alignments
- **Microcopy**: Full European Portuguese (PT-PT) grammar check. Eradicated Brazil-specific expressions (e.g. "você", "nossa", "seu") in favor of "sua empresa" and "a organização".
- **SEO Metadata**: Created custom meta descriptions and Open Graph cards to maximize search CTR.
